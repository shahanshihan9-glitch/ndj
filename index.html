What is Novari?

Novari is a free, kid-safe learning app you can use in any browser — no download, no sign-up, no cost. Novari is a friendly star robot who chats with children, answers their questions in simple language, and turns learning into a game.

Inside the app there are ten things to explore: Chat with Novari, a Cartoon Explorer with quizzes, a History section with a Second World War timeline, a World Religions guide covering Islam, Christianity, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism and Sikhism, Languages in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Arabic, Sinhala and Tamil, a Game Mode, a School section with maths, science and English by school grade, a Talk page where Novari reads answers out loud, a private Diary, and Stickers to collect.

Novari is built for children roughly aged 5 to 17, works on phones, tablets and computers, and keeps a kind, encouraging tone with bad-word filtering built in. Novari is a pretend robot buddy — children are always reminded to check important things with a grown-up.

🌟 Privacy & Safety

For kids 👋
  • 🤖 Novari is an AI — a computer helper, not a real person.
  • 🤫 Never tell Novari your full name, home address, school, phone number or passwords.
  • 💛 If something makes you sad or scared, talk to a grown-up you trust.
  • 📓 Your diary and stickers stay on this device only.

For parents and guardians

Novari is a free learning app for children. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no sign-up. Most of Novari works entirely inside your child's browser.

What happens to your child's information

InformationWhere it goes
Nickname, birthday, country, language (welcome screen)Saved only on this device so Novari remembers who is playing (tap "Change player" under the chat to remove it). Never sent anywhere.
Diary entries, stickers, progressSaved in this browser on this device only. We never receive them.
Everything typed or said to Novari in chat, and written exam answersSent to Novari's own AI server, which runs open-source AI models on Cloudflare's network to create a reply. Before sending, Novari removes email addresses, phone numbers, links and the child's nickname. We do not store these messages.
Internet address (IP)Turned into a scrambled one-way code to stop overuse, kept up to 2 days, then deleted automatically. The real IP is never stored.
Voice (Talk tab)Speech-to-text is handled by your browser or device maker (for example Google or Apple) under their privacy terms. Novari only receives the typed text.

Safety measures

Novari follows strict child-safety rules, answers briefly in simple language, refuses unsafe topics, and tells children to speak to a trusted adult when they seem upset. A word filter checks both what children type and what the AI replies. No AI is perfect, so we encourage grown-ups to explore Novari together with younger children.

Your choices

You can delete diary entries inside the app, or remove everything by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
