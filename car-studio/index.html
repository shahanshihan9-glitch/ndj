Before you upgrade

Your questions, answered.

Will an Android player fit my vehicle?

Fit depends on your vehicle model, dashboard frame and wiring. Tell us your make, model and year so our team can advise before you order.

What warranty is included?

The YD and Kenwood players list a 3-month warranty. The Lenovo D1 4GB / 64GB player lists a 1-year warranty. A warranty was not specified for every other product; ask us to confirm the exact cover and terms.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Cash on delivery is available for the listed Android players. Ask our team to confirm availability for other products, your address and any delivery charges before ordering.

Is installation included in the product price?

Installation is available for the listed Android players. The snail-shaped horn lists installation included. Ask us to confirm labour, fitting accessories and the final installed price for your vehicle.

Does the 30% offer mean another discount on the listed prices?

The banner advertises the Android-player offer and the catalogue shows the supplied special prices. Please confirm the final offer with the team; this website does not apply an additional automatic discount.

Can I book a confirmed appointment on this website?

You can prepare an installation request with a preferred date and send it through WhatsApp. Your slot is only confirmed after the Car Studio team replies.
